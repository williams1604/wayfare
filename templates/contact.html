Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about our services

How quickly will I receive a response?

We typically respond to all inquiries within 24 hours during business days. For urgent matters, please call our hotline for immediate assistance.

Can I modify my booking inquiry?

Absolutely! Simply send us a follow-up email or call us directly with your updated preferences. We're flexible and here to help.

Do you offer in-person consultations?

Yes, we welcome visitors to our office. We recommend scheduling an appointment to ensure a travel consultant is available to meet with you.

What information should I include?

The more details you provide about your destination, dates, budget, and preferences, the better we can tailor our recommendations to your needs.

Is there a consultation fee?

All initial consultations and travel planning services are completely free. You only pay when you're ready to book your perfect vacation.

Do you handle group bookings?

Yes! We specialize in group travel, corporate retreats, and destination events. Contact us for special group rates and customized itineraries.
